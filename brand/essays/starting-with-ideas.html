Starting with ideas

Most brands start with a logo and a price list. This one starts with ideas: essays, breakdowns of brands I admire, campaign concepts and notes on what I read.

For the first year there’s no newsletter, no accounts and nothing for sale. Just the work, in public, so anyone can see how I think about brands before I ever ask them for anything.

If it turns into a studio one day, this is where it started.
