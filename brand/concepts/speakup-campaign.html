Say it out loud: a campaign for SpeakUp

Concept, not commissioned work.

SpeakUp is my offline app for practising spoken English. This is a concept for how I’d launch it.

The insight

People don’t fail to speak a language because they lack words. They fail because they never practise out loud, and most apps let you avoid it.

The idea

Say it out loud. Short films of people practising in the places they actually have a spare minute: a bus stop, a queue, a lift. No signal needed, no account, just a card, a timer and your own voice.
