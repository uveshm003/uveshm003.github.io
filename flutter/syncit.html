SyncIT

A cross-platform Flutter platform for Sensit’s gas-detection instruments that consolidates several legacy apps into one product, in two tiers: Lite for offline field use and Plus with cloud sync, reporting and integrations.

ROLE
Primary developer, owning product decisions with no dedicated product role

PLATFORMS
Android, iOS, Windows
KIND
Work
STATUS
PRIVATE

The problem

Sensit’s gas-detection instruments were served by several separate legacy apps. Field users switched between them to download logs, read the data and export reports, and changing an instrument’s settings in production was done by hand, one menu at a time.

What I did

  • Took SyncIT from a proof of concept to a production MVP, owning scope, tiering and release readiness.
  • Consolidated the legacy apps into one Flutter product for Android, iOS and Windows, on a shared core package and log-parsing SDK.
  • Split it into two tiers on one codebase: Lite, free and offline-first for field use, and Plus, paid, with cloud sync, advanced reporting and integrations.
  • Proposed and built an automated settings tool that applies 50+ instrument settings over BLE, with the firmware team.
  • Coordinated requirements and releases across teams in India, Italy and the US.

What changed

One app instead of several, a clear free and paid split, and roughly 99% less manual effort when instruments are configured in production. Reworking the local database schema and queries made the app about 30-35% faster.
