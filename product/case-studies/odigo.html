The write-up

The product

Odigo is an advertising robot with two mirrored displays and central control, built at Kody Technolabs for a client.

What I did

I built the robot’s Flutter app and wired it to the hardware through ROS SDK, platform channels and Socket.IO.

Where it went

The company showed Odigo at GITEX 2024 and deployed it at LuLu Mall, DLF Mall and Jio World Drive.
